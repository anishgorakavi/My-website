## Selected Projects & Research Areas

### DNS Security / DNS Posture Management

Anish has explored an outside-in DNS Security Posture Management concept focused on understanding an organization's Internet-facing infrastructure from an attacker's perspective.

Areas explored include:

- DNS exposure and posture
- DNS → IP → ASN relationships
- BGP routing visibility
- Prefix and ASN inventory
- RPKI / ROA validation
- Route changes and withdrawals
- Prefix hygiene
- DDoS protection intent vs. routing reality
- Infrastructure drift
- Blast-radius modeling

The larger idea is to connect DNS, routing, ASN, and security context into a coherent external view of infrastructure risk.

### AI Security Posture Management

Anish has worked on concepts around AI Security Posture Management (AISPM), including visibility into AI services, vendors, accounts, policies, traffic, and organizational AI usage.

A recurring design model is:

**Visibility → Observability → Guided Remediation**

Areas of interest include sanctioned vs. unsanctioned AI usage, vendor governance, service inventory, traffic and policy visibility, and unified AI risk dashboards.

### Agentic AI + MCP

Anish has been experimenting with agentic AI architectures and the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

He is particularly interested in MCP as an interoperability layer that allows AI models and agents to interact with tools, security systems, and structured capabilities without requiring every integration to invent its own interface.

Project concepts have included:

- Agentic OSINT threat research
- AI-assisted vulnerability management
- Cybersecurity career advising
- Education safety agents
- Multi-agent security analysis
- Model routing and orchestration

### AI EduShield

AI EduShield is a concept for a browser-based safety layer for K–12 AI usage. It uses specialized agents to evaluate risks such as:

- Sensitive information / PII exposure
- Academic-integrity concerns
- Harmful or abusive content
- Student safety signals

The broader architectural idea uses policy-controlled agent orchestration to make AI use safer in managed educational environments.

### AI Cybersecurity Career Advisor

A system concept designed to help students navigate cybersecurity careers by connecting interests and skills with structured career frameworks and possible learning paths.

The project grew from Anish's interest in making cybersecurity opportunities more accessible to teenagers who may not have access to industry mentors or specialized guidance.

### ID.JS / IDexe

Anish explored approaches to making browser sessions harder to hijack by binding session context more closely to a device.

ID.JS explored generating a relatively stable device-derived identifier, while IDexe explored moving sensitive session state outside the browser and validating it through a localhost agent.

These were proof-of-concept explorations rather than claims of production-ready authentication architecture.

### PhishLake

In 2023, Anish built a phishing-data aggregation project called **PhishLake**. It collected data from sources such as PhishTank, Google Safe Browsing, and VirusTotal and maintained an automatically updated phishing dataset.

The project helped expose the constant detection/evasion cycle between phishing infrastructure and reputation systems.
